# Enriched ledger schema and boundaries

`cluster_ledger.csv` is a generated UTF-8 CSV with provenance rows for every local card and one `compound_card` row per synthesis-card compound. Counts change as cards change. JSON-array columns are compact JSON strings; parse them as arrays, not comma-delimited text. Empty scalar means missing/unknown, not zero or ambient.

- `compound_key = DOI#compound[index]` is a stable key *for this card ordering*, not a chemical identifier. `compound_index` is zero-based. `local_card_path` and `source_path` locate the skill-folder-relative JSON; no card copies are made. `source_doi`, `card_id`, `ccdc_number`, and `refcode` retain provenance. On compound rows, `ccdc_number` and `refcode` are JSON arrays because a compound may list multiple identifiers; `LOCAL_...` is not a deposit.
- Composition: `nuclearity` comes from `material_class.nuclearity` (reported total metals, **not necessarily rare-earth count**); `rare_earth_elements`, `other_metal_elements`, `all_metal_elements`, `metal_composition`, `dimensionality`. Missing or inconsistent counts require source verification.
- Synthesis: `reagent_names`, `reagent_formulas`, `ligand_reagent_names`, `solvents` are JSON arrays. The ligand subset requires a reagent role containing the word *ligand*, which is incomplete when source roles are omitted. `synthesis_procedure`, `temperature`, `time`, `ph`, `vessel_or_method`, `workup_and_crystallization`, `yield`, `synthesis_in_main_text`, `si_used` are compound-level card fields, not validated lab protocols.
- Structure: `structural_family` and `structure_description` reflect article extraction; `structure_terms` contains literal English shape keywords found in those fields, not canonical topological labels. Compound rows also list `linked_structure_refcodes`, CCDC numbers, candidate motif statuses and module metal-composition summaries from matched CIF cards. Structure rows carry crystal system, space group, formula moiety, graph availability, CIF-derived topology/shape, `candidate_modules` JSON and `candidate_module_compositions`, `geometry_basis`, and `interpretation_limit`. Algorithmic candidate motifs remain hypotheses, especially with missing atom/bond data or omitted symmetry edges.
- Local CIF identifiers: `extracted_cards/*` uses `list_of_rare_earth_compounds[*].ccdc_refcodes` for both genuine CSD refcodes and locally assigned `LOCAL_...` identifiers. A `LOCAL_...` value matches the filename stem and `refcode` of `cif_structure_cards/LOCAL_....json`; it is **not** a CCDC refcode or number. The local CIF may originate in the article/SI without a CCDC deposit reported in the card (`ccdc_numbers: []`, structure-card `ccdc_number: null`). Exact DOI-scoped local linkage can still provide CIF-derived evidence. Check `source_cif` and original article/SI for provenance and compound identity; do not infer a database deposit or categorical absence of one from this marker. In the ledger, compound `refcode` and `linked_structure_refcodes` may therefore contain local identifiers.
- Links: Structure-card DOI provenance is established during production outside this skill; within that DOI, compound links require exact card CCDC/refcode identifiers. Multiple candidate structure cards for one identifier or multiple compounds claiming the same structure card are listed in the published `compound_structure_review.csv` snapshot. The affected compound rows are `ambiguous_local_cif_match` with empty linked-structure arrays, pending manual review. `matched_local_cif_card` means a locally resolved exact match; `no_local_cif_match` means no resolved local card, **not** proof of CIF absence. DOI alone never creates a compound–CIF link. `review_card` rows index scope-aware review claims but are not joined to compounds.
- Property fields (`property_types`, `property_summary`) index reported card content, not full measurement conditions. For comparisons, open `properties.measurements` in the source card.

Recommended retrieval: filter `compound_card` rows by element + reagent/ligand + nuclearity, then narrow by article structural family/shape; follow `linked_structure_refcodes` to structure rows, check `match_cardinality`/graph status, and inspect only selected original cards. For structural analogs, start with same article family and nuclearity, then compare metal composition and shape; shared words or similar nuclearity alone do **not** establish isostructurality.

For compounds with no resolved local structure, answer article-level `structure_description`, formula and reported family if present, with a clear source label. State that CIF-derived connectivity, cell/space group or symmetry-complete topology cannot be verified from this corpus. Never infer details from the presence of a CCDC number alone.



Structure-card rows set `source_doi` from the DOI-level file reconciliation snapshot; this is provenance, not a compound-level match. `cluster_ledger.csv` is the skill's query interface, distinct from the file-level local-linkage report. `search_ledger.py` filters compound rows only; structure-only CCDC/refcodes must be inspected in the ledger's structure rows.
